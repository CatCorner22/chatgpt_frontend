# What a blind booking trial can tell us

Focus’s North Star is frontend work as good as a world-class human designer and coder would deliver. A person can prefer a page, misidentify its source, and still struggle to finish a booking. Focus needs a separate observation for each of these questions.

## What the studies measured

- On Contra’s 33 briefs, 35 designers cast 4,311 votes. Human reference designs won 425 of the 574 head-to-head votes in which they appeared (74%). The 34-part rubric tracked the ranking across 15 AI models closely, yet ranked the human references sixth of 16. The median brief length of 138 words and the 75% with multiple constraints describe a separate corpus of roughly 4,600 job postings. There is no evidence here for a word-count minimum in Focus. [Frontier Design Benchmark](https://contralabs.com/research/frontier-design-benchmark)
- In the November 25 snapshot of Real Design Benchmark, participants correctly identified the AI screen in 58% of rounds. Preference was 56% for the AI screen, 33% for the shipped screen, and 11% equal. These were self-selected votes on static feature screens drawn from existing products, mostly business software. The comparison supplies neither a marketing-page benchmark nor an observed task-completion rate. [Results](https://www.realdesignbenchmark.com/results) · [Methodology](https://www.realdesignbenchmark.com/methodology)
- FigmaEval reported 76.58% agreement with designers on pairs of AI-created Figma designs using a 22-item rubric. An ICSE study comparing ratings of 84 generated prototypes with 39 HCI experts found poor agreement for GPT-4o. Use concrete critique prompts, identify the assessor, and retain the person’s own verdict. [FigmaEval](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.32321v1) · [Rate My Web Design?](https://memorias.lifia.ar/publications/bakaev-2026)
- Han Kim describes 27 candidate visual signals calibrated on 202 selected design-led sites. This establishes candidate warnings. Thresholds for blocking a customer delivery need a separate held-out sample and a count of good designs incorrectly flagged. HumanSignal’s 4,500 votes compared model outputs with other model outputs. Solo Design’s six-prompt teardown offers examples of filler copy to inspect. Neither establishes the prevalence of a given pattern in human work. [The Tells](https://labs.iovstudio.kr/papers/ai-design-tells.pdf) · [HumanSignal](https://humansignal.com/blog/ai-has-a-house-style-we-measured-it-across-10-labs/) · [Solo Design](https://solodesign.cc/blog/ai-design-slop-the-tells/)
- In Selim’s record of one designer’s 242 corrections, 173 came after a rule in that family existed. In 92 of those cases the rule lived only in a different product’s repository. Focus can address that failure directly by delivering the owner’s requirements with the actual project and naming the check that demonstrates each requirement. [Beyond the Default](https://yousofselim.com/research/beyond-the-default/)
- In the visual-refinement study, 17.8% was the best-candidate improvement for one model on an automated visual judge. Its mean third iteration improved by 8.7%. UI2Code-N studies reconstruction and editing of supplied screenshots. Focus already captures local screenshots and supports visual comparison. Keep before/after images with the functional checks so a visual repair that breaks an interaction is visible. Paid screenshot critique remains a separate decision. [Vision-Guided Iterative Refinement, Table 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.05839) · [UI2Code-N](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/yang26v.html)
- Startrise combines browser checks, a model panel, and in-house human ratings, with uneven human coverage across submissions. Focus can borrow its practice of checking the working page while publishing the denominator for each kind of evidence. [Startrise benchmark](https://www.startrise.io/benchmark/)

## What changed in Focus

The ready-to-run River Kiln study presents two working booking pages under neutral labels. It collects first choice, price, help, time, recovery, and the saved reservation count. After exporting the primary answers, reviewers can cast a separate preference ballot. The organizer can check the ballot against the exact primary file. Missing ballots, no preference, and inability to compare have different meanings and are counted separately. The two samples are the original practice app and its Focus redesign. An attribution guess in this pilot cannot establish AI-versus-human accuracy. [Run the study](RIVER-KILN-PILOT.md)

## What to build next

1. **Trace owner requirements to delivered evidence.** Reuse the Garden brief and its existing handoff. Give each explicit constraint an owner, an affected screen or state, and an observable acceptance check. Carry the trace in the exported brief. A supplied logo or a checked box alone cannot establish that the deployed page used it.
2. **Calibrate advisory craft checks.** Start from Focus’s existing six rating dimensions, copy warnings, and browser receipts. For each candidate signal record a concrete example, a counterexample, the pages examined, and the false-positive count on held-out, permissioned human work. Reserve blocking behavior for demonstrable failures such as a broken destination or an explicit owner requirement.
3. **Run a matched expert comparison.** Start with one permissioned independent expert build on the same brief and assets. Reuse the six existing benchmark tasks when expanding beyond booking. Record source provenance, assistance, missing observations and denominators. Keep exploratory pilot results separate from a preregistered equivalence study.

## Reading the follow-up findings

The follow-up describes an image comparison with a verdict after 20 decided votes. As an illustration, 10 correct identifications out of 20 independent votes has a 95% Wilson interval of about 30% to 70%. That result would still allow a substantial difference from chance. Plan a practical equivalence margin and sample size in advance, and report attribution and preference separately. The live Focus review uses working pages in the Review Room. There are no human votes in its automated fixtures.

The report also describes zero craft warnings across eight selected homepages inspected through their source HTML. That is a useful smoke test for false alarms. A blocking rule would need known failures it can catch and good rendered pages it can clear. Until then, keep those signals advisory. One interaction that can be tested directly is whether the final words of a draft survive pressing Back, including after a temporary storage failure. The repeatable browser check is in [test-autosave-navigation.mjs](../scripts/test-autosave-navigation.mjs).

## Two checks on what a customer receives

**Can someone read and navigate the page in the language the owner chose?** Focus previously exported every page with `lang="en"`, including pages written in Japanese. In the working-page editor, **Page language** now sets the default language of the document and the language of built-in labels such as the skip link, contents and next step. Choose the predominant language of the page. Customer wording, image descriptions and destinations stay as entered. Japanese output uses strict line breaking and the visitor's installed fonts for Japanese glyphs. W3C explains how assistive technology can use the language declaration to select pronunciation. A browser check can verify the declaration and keyboard path. Actual pronunciation requires listening with a screen reader. [W3C language of page](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/language-of-page.html) · [CSS line breaking](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-text-3/#line-break-property)

**Does information survive when someone takes it away?** In a Product layout with three descriptions, Chromium's original Save as PDF included only the open one. The print layout now presents the descriptions in order, including those folded shut on screen. The print test generates an A4 PDF from each delivery format, extracts its text and checks the descriptions, their order and the absence of duplicates. It then returns to the screen and operates the disclosures using the keyboard. This measures Chromium's PDF output on the fixture. Other print engines and physical printers need their own checks. [Print styles](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Guides/Media_queries/Printing) · [Disclosure contents](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Selectors/::details-content)

To repeat the language and handoff checks, run `npm run test:authored` with `FOCUS_TEST_URL` pointing at the running Focus instance. The Japanese rendering check needs a Japanese system font. Run `npm run test:product-print` with Chromium and Poppler's `pdftotext` installed to verify the words in both PDFs.
